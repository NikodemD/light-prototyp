News

Items marked “sample” only show the layout of a future story. They do not report events that have already happened.

  • 27–30.09.2026 · Meetings

    Consortium meeting in Lund

    On 27–30 September 2026 the LIGHT partners met in Lund. The programme included working sessions, a presentation of the DALEC tools and a walk through the city.

  • 17.08.2026 · Fieldwork

    Drones and a 3D model of Martigny

    Gdańsk Tech and Idiap scanned Martigny with drones and photogrammetry. The material will become a 3D model of the city, for daylight studies and the partners’ simulations.

  • 10.02.2026 · Meetings

    Gdańsk Tech launches LIGHT

    On 10 February 2026 the Faculty of Architecture launched the international LIGHT project. It works on healthy Positive Energy Districts. Gdańsk Tech is the lead partner.
