LIGHT aims to set a pattern for healthy, efficient and inclusive Positive Energy Districts. The results below are planned. They are not published yet.

Transformation framework

A model other European cities can reuse.

LIGHT Guidelines

Public, practical guidance for integrated urban planning.

Decision-support toolkit

Open software based on CitySim and DALEC. CitySim is to cover indoor daylight and indoor and outdoor thermal comfort. DALEC is to cover shading and daylight in dense neighbourhoods. Investors, planners and local authorities are the users.
